# Literature data used for the out-of-sample tests (checked 2026-10-18)

All mouse values are from BALB/c small intestine (terminal ileum in 2007) in nifedipine 3 µM, intracellular recording, ICC-MY and
circular smooth muscle (CSMC) recorded in **different tissues** (unpaired). Means ± SD; n = animals unless stated.
Source column: T = text, Tab = table, Fig = digitized from a bar chart (400-dpi render of the PDF, bar ends located by pixel runs
against the axis ticks; read precision about ±0.3 mV and ±0.02 V/s; SD whiskers not digitized reliably).

## Guinea-pig antrum (Cousins et al. 2003, J Physiol 550:829) — not an independent test

Resistances were solved from electrotonic potentials together with the spontaneous pacemaker and initial-component amplitudes
(V4, V5 as inputs), so agreement of a divider with those amplitudes is partly by construction. Their own circuit model gave a
~12 mV initial component and a ~20 mV follower potential for a 40 mV pacemaker potential (recorded 15.8, 23.4 vs 42.7 mV).
