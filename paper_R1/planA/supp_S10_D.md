## Supplement S10. The published ICC model against freshly prepared ICC

The ICC model of [15] (25 pF, without the changes made in this study) was compared with the freshly prepared ICC on which it was built [19], using the recording conditions of [19] where possible. The model reproduces the resting potential and the capacitance; it differs in the input resistance and its rectification, the rate of rise and peak of the pacemaker potential, and the spontaneous currents under voltage clamp at a constant potential (Tables S14–S16). The comparison of the input resistance (Table S15) sets the range of resting-conductance scale factors used for models D and C.

**Table S14.** The published ICC model (isolated, 25 pF, unmodified) against freshly prepared ICC [19] (35–36 °C, perforated patch).

| Quantity | Freshly prepared ICC [19] | Model |
|---|---|---|

| Peak of the pacemaker potential | −1.6 ± 3.8 mV | −14.4 mV |
| Frequency | 26.8 ± 6.2 min⁻¹ | 17.2 min⁻¹ |
| Duration at 50% repolarization | 553 ± 174 ms | 775 ms |
| Maximum rate of rise | 6.7 ± 2.5 V/s at −26.3 mV | 1.24 V/s at −36 mV |
| Input resistance, holding −80 mV (Table S15) | 0.76 ± 0.41 GΩ (n = 3) | 77 MΩ (−20-mV step) to 271 MΩ (+20-mV step) |
| Rectification | slight inward rectification | conductance 3.5 times larger for −20 than for +20 mV |
| Spontaneous currents at a constant holding potential (Table S16) | mostly no full-size inward currents; small currents < 100 pA, tens of ms | full-size inward currents of about 440 pA every 3.6 s at −80 mV |
