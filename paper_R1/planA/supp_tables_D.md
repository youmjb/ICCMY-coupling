**Reducing the resting conductance of ICC-MY.** A negative passive conductance at −72 mV (−1 to −3 nS) was added to ICC-MY of the original-conductance model in the active-muscle column. At −1 nS the cell either latched near +20 mV (g\_c 1 nS) or, at g\_c 1.5–2 nS, paced but exploded with apamin; at −2 and −3 nS it did not pace or the integration diverged. A lower own conductance therefore needs a different balance of the resting currents, not a simple leak change: scaling the resting conductances and the transporters that balance them together (Table S9) lowers the own conductance while the isolated cell keeps pacing down to f = 0.2.
